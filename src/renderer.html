“A limit does not necessarily ask what happens at a point.
It asks what happens to the function as we get closer and closer to that point.”
Linear Reference Curve
Identical to y = x + 2 everywhere
except a removable hole at (2, 4)!
Tracing Both Directions
From the left (x → 2⁻) → 4
From the right (x → 2⁺) → 4
Both sides approach the same value!
Step 1 • Direct Substitution
0/0 → Indeterminate Form! (Does NOT mean limit is 0 — simplify!)
Step 2 • Factor the Numerator
Difference of squares: a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b)
Step 3 • Rewrite & Spot Common Factor
Highlight the common factor (x − 2) in numerator & denominator
Step 4 • Cancel Common Factor (x ≠ 2)
Valid because as x → 2, x ≠ 2, so (x − 2) ≠ 0
